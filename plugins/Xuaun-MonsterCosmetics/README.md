# MonsterCosmetics

Wear the **monsters of R.E.P.O.** as cosmetics! This pack carves hats, faces, bodywear, shoes and full body meshes straight out of the game's own enemy models and drops them into the **vanilla cosmetics menu** — almost 70 cosmetics from 22 different monsters.

## Reactive behaviours & physics

Some cosmetics do more than just sit there:

- **Bomb & dynamite (Bang)** — gently pulse, growing and shrinking in place.
- **Balloon (Birthday Boy)** — takes damage as you get hit, **pops with a sound** once it takes enough, then re-inflates after a short delay. Pops stay in sync across players in multiplayer.
- **Balloon (Birthday Boy)** — now you can choose the balloon's color in the config (using the other ingame colors or a custom color).
- **Spring (jiggle) physics** — dangly pieces sway and bounce with your movement instead of staying rigid.

## Installation

1. Install with **Thunderstore Mod Manager** or **r2modman** — dependencies are resolved automatically.
2. Launch the game. The cosmetics appear in the in-game cosmetics menu.

**Manual install:** extract into `BepInEx/plugins/` and make sure [REPOLib](https://thunderstore.io/c/repo/p/Zehs/REPOLib/) is installed.
