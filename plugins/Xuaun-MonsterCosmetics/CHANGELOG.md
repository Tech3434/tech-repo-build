## 1.1.0

### Added — reactive behaviours & spring physics

- **Bomb & dynamite pulse:** Bang's **bomb** (bodywear top) and **dynamite** (body bottom) now "breathe", smoothly growing and shrinking in place. Configurable speed and strength.
- **Popping balloon:** Birthday Boy's **balloon** (bodywear top) takes damage as you get hit — once it reaches enough hits or accumulated damage it **pops with a sound**, then re-inflates after a short delay. Pops stay in sync across players in multiplayer. Hits-to-pop, damage-to-pop, respawn and re-inflate times are all configurable.
- **Spring (jiggle) physics:** added spring/jiggle physics to dangly cosmetics across many monsters, so they sway and bounce with your movement.
