# Manufacturer of Style
### This level was a Commission by Crocs

The Manufacturer of Style is a new level where you will be collecting various types of Crocs in an expansive store and warehouse.<br>
The crocs found here have several different effects and the environment gets more and more dilapidated the further you go.<br>

"Collecting, extracting and fighting monsters can be exhausting… but now there’s a way to do it in total comfort. Slip into the comfiest shoes ever made, inspired by Crocs' iconic Classic Clog and get buffs you never imagined." <br>

### It currently has 52 unique rooms and 28 unique valuables

Like other base game valuables some of these can have adverse effects on you or the enemies. <br>
