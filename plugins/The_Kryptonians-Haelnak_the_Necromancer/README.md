# Haelnak the Necromancer

Something tall and hooded glides through the level, a hand's breadth off the floor. You'll hear it before you see
it: a low hum, cloth dragging, a voice muttering to itself in the dark.

It doesn't need to come close. It would rather watch you from across the room and take what it wants from there.

And when it takes everything, it isn't finished with you. Your body has other plans.

## Found taped inside the truck

It doesn't hurry. It doesn't have to. When its hand comes up green the air goes thin and the warmth starts leaving
you for it, though it can't drink what it can't see. The dark it throws is slow; it never feels slow. It flinches when
you hurt it, not for long. We heard it chanting over Dex and nobody stopped it. Dex got up and walked off, and when we
saw him again he had his face back, sat on his shoulders like it had never left. Mira took hold of it and pulled. What
came away was still Dex. What fell down wasn't. It walks the halls watching the floor now, so we don't leave anything
lying around, not anything that used to be one of us. And when we brought Dex back properly, the other one just came
apart.
