### **Cats, Cats, and more Cats!**

![cats](https://i.imgur.com/hxZzpn4.png)

## Features

* Adds 70+ different **cats/cat related** valuables. (Most are animated)
* Adds 10 cat related **Items** to shop and attic

  * [Catcake](https://storage.ko-fi.com/cdn/useruploads/display/530ecc41-2437-4652-915e-1244e5a7ced0_screenshot2026-10-28122714.png) - Will heal 50hp and make you 1.5x faster in the next level.
  * [Meoffin](https://storage.ko-fi.com/cdn/useruploads/display/fba75e87-5cd6-498a-8bbe-c381dbf1b376_screenshot2026-10-28122740.png) - Will heal 25hp and make you quieter in the next level.
  * [Rubber Cat](https://storage.ko-fi.com/cdn/useruploads/display/8feb5aab-2265-4c29-a2ca-776300ec4c35_screenshot2026-11-08234332.png) - throw it. MEOW.
  * [Lazer Pointer 9000](https://i.imgur.com/YUKYFPF.mp4) - The laser pointer reimagined as a powerful laser gun with strong tumble force.
  * [Bastet Staff](https://i.imgur.com/ccBbJ7J.mp4) - A staff that uses that power of Bastet to call upon all other cats to attack the target.
  * Cat On The Cob (WIP) - A cat eating a corn on the cob. Slightly stronger than the sledgehammer. Animated. Temporarily sounds like the sledgehammer.
  * [Pat](https://i.imgur.com/JPtkzkW.mp4) - Paw bat with sharp metal claws. Same as a bat with double damage.
  * [Yarn Ball Grenade](https://i.imgur.com/8GWzbze.mp4) - Explodes and sends strings of yarn flying everywhere. If a player or enemy is hit by the string, they will be slowed for 8 seconds. Has 11 variants.
  * [Pocket B.E.D.](https://i.imgur.com/lsCUwdY.mp4) - A cat bed that is used as a pocket C.A.R.T. Will heal any players that are tumbling (press Q) inside of it.
  * [Cat Box](https://i.imgur.com/JJ6S27r.mp4) - A cardboard box that can be used to trap small enemies or to hide from enemies. Same as duck bucket with added benefit of being able to see out of it when hiding



* **Controlled Spawning**, allowing you to control the spawning of the cats. (Keep off if you prefer regular spawning)

  * Control how many cats spawn
  * Control how often certain rarities are spawned
* **Custom naming**, allowing you to name the cats you find. Will use names from *everyone's* config files. You can have multiple names for same cat by separating with a comma.

## Config

**Requires** [REPOConfig](https://thunderstore.io/c/repo/p/nickklmao/REPOConfig/) **in order to have in menu mod settings.** Otherwise, config file will be located in Bepinex/config folder.

|Key|Default|Description|
|-|-|-|
|`Controlled Spawning`|`Off`|Activates every setting under Controlled Spawning|
|`Spawn Count`|`3`|How many cats are forced to spawn|
|`Common Weight`|`90`|How often common cats spawn|
|`Uncommon Weight`|`60`|How often uncommon cats spawn|
|`Rare Weight`|`20`|How often rare cats spawn|
|`Custom Weight`|`50`|How often commissioned cats spawn|
|`Cat Type`|`Example, Example2, Example3`|Randomly chooses a name for each cat type. Separate multiple names with a comma. Blank by **default**|
